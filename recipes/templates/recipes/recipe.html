Homemade Pizza Recipe

{#change Homemade Pizza Recipe to {{recipe.name}}#}
Cuisine: Italian Dietary Info: Vegetarian
{# #}

Servings: 4 to 6

Prep Time: 30 mins

Cook Time: 30 mins

Total Time: 2 hrs 30 min

Ingredients

Pizza Dough

  • 1 1/2 cups warm water (105 to 115 °F)
  • 1 package (2 1/4 teaspoons) active dry yeast
  • 3 3/4 cups (490g) bread flour
  • 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 teaspoons kosher salt
  • 1 teaspoon sugar

Pizza and toppings

  • Extra olive oil
  • Cornmeal
  • Tomato sauce
  • Firm mozzarella cheese, separated into small clumps
  • Fontina cheese, grated
  • Parmesan cheese, grated
  • Feta cheese, crumbled
  • Mushrooms, very thinly sliced if raw, otherwise first sauteed
  • Bell peppers, stems and seeds removed, very thinly sliced
  • Italian pepperoncini, thinly sliced
  • Italian sausage, cooked ahead and crumbled
  • Sliced black olives
  • Chopped fresh basil
  • Baby arugula, tossed in a little olive oil, added as pizza comes out of the oven
  • Pesto
  • Pepperoni, thinly sliced
  • Onions, thinly sliced raw or caramelized
  • Ham, thinly sliced

Instructions

Make the dough

  1. Pour warm water into a stand mixer bowl, sprinkle the yeast on top, and let it sit 5 minutes until foamy. (If you have instant yeast, skip this and add it straight to the flour.)
  2. Add flour, salt, sugar, and olive oil. Mix on low with the paddle for 1 minute.
  3. Switch to the dough hook and knead on low-medium for 7-10 minutes. (Or knead by hand.)
  4. The dough should be slightly tacky. If it’s too wet, add a little flour.

Let it rise

  1. Coat a large bowl with olive oil, add the dough, and turn it to coat. Cover with plastic wrap.
  2. Choose your rise time:
    • Quick: 1½ hours in a warm spot (75-85°F)
    • Medium: 8 hours on the counter
    • Slow: 24 hours in the fridge (48 hours max), for the best flavor

Prep and shape

  1. Put a pizza stone (or sturdy baking sheet or pizza pan) on a lower oven rack. Preheat to 475°F for 30-60 minutes.
  2. Punch the dough down gently and divide it in half. Shape into 2 balls, cover, and rest 15 minutes (up to 2 hours).
  3. Prep toppings, going light so the crust stays crisp. About ⅓ cup each of sauce and cheese per pizza is plenty
  4. On a floured surface, press one ball out from the center to ½ inch thick, then stretch it.
  5. Let it rest 5 minutes, then stretch to 10-12 inches. Be gentle, and pinch any holes closed.
  6. Press dents into the dough with your fingertips and brush the top with olive oil. Rest 10-15 minutes.
  7. Repeat with the second ball.

Assemble and bake

  1. Sprinkle a pizza peel (or flat baking sheet) with cornmeal and move the dough onto it.
  2. Add sauce, cheese, and toppings.
  3. Sprinkle a little cornmeal on the hot stone. Shake the peel to check the pizza slides, adding more cornmeal if it sticks.
  4. Slide the pizza onto the stone.
  5. Bake 10-15 minutes, one pizza at a time, until the crust is browned and the cheese is golden.

Notes

  • Leftover dough keeps in the fridge for up to 3 days. Wrap it tightly in oiled plastic wrap.
  • Go light on toppings. Too many will make the center soggy.
